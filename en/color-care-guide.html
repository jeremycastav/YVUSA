How to choose your color

  1. Look at your ends

    Compare against your ends, not your roots: that’s where the extension blends in.

  2. Use daylight

    Take a photo by a window, with no filters or flash.

  3. Ask us

    Send us the photo on WhatsApp and we’ll recommend a shade and technique. [confirm service]

Yesenia’s tips

How to care for your extensions

  • Wash every other day or daily, depending on your hair. Never several times a day: they dry out.
  • Use moisturizing and protective products, always from mid-lengths to ends.
  • It’s inert hair: nothing nourishes it from the root, so it needs more care than your own hair.
  • Book maintenance every two to three months, depending on the technique.
  • They are not recommended if you are being treated for a scalp condition.

Sources: Yesenia Vargas quoted in El Dominguero (February 5, 2023) and Mujer · Diario Súper (November 13, 2021).
